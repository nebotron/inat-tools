Taxonomic tree

Interactive cladogram from iNaturalist taxonomy: root at the top, lineages downward. Pan and zoom, click a node’s ring to fold or unfold, and double-click an internal node to zoom that subtree. Where NCBI taxonomy can be matched, junction ages from TimeTree of Life appear along branches (million years ago, with published confidence intervals when available). ← Observation browser

Species on the tree

    Tree

    Large branches start folded when a node covers more than five of your selected species (the top-level taxon stays expanded). Scroll or pinch to zoom; drag the canvas to pan. Click the transparent ring on a node to fold or unfold; double-click an internal node to zoom its subtree. Hover a label for the full name. Purple labels along branches are TimeTree MRCA ages when both sides map to NCBI ids (via Open Tree TNRS).

    Branch geometry follows iNaturalist taxonomy, not branch lengths. Divergence times are median MRCA estimates from the TimeTree of Life database (Kumar et al.), using NCBI taxonomy ids resolved with the Open Tree of Life TNRS service. If your browser blocks cross-origin access to TimeTree, the app may load those JSON responses through a public CORS relay (allorigins.win). Data from iNaturalist.
